      What this map does not show
      • Routes with fewer than test flights are drawn as thin dashed lines and left out of rankings: rates on a handful of flights are noise.
      • Accuracy (AUC) is only calculated for routes with 300+ flights.
      • Delay is measured at departure from New York. Nothing here says the destination airport caused it.
      • The model uses observed weather, which a live system would only have as a forecast.
      • This is 2013 New York data; it says nothing about Heathrow or any other airport.
